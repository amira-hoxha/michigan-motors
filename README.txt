Michigan Motors — Website Source

Open index.html in your browser, or run:
  python3 -m http.server 8080
Then visit http://localhost:8080

No build step or dependencies required. HTML pages, style.css, app.js and local image assets are included.

Includes five palette options, persistent palette selection, animated scroll lines, vehicle filters, favorites, comparison, quick views, and demo forms.
Google Fonts requires an internet connection. Forms are demonstration only and do not send information. Inventory and pricing are illustrative.

Edit style.css for appearance, app.js for vehicle data and interactions, and the HTML files for page content.
