A few good
questions.

Will this affect my credit?

No. The calculator only performs a calculation in your browser. No credit check or application takes place.

What does the estimate include?

Vehicle price minus down payment, your entered APR and the selected loan term. Taxes, fees, insurance and optional products are excluded.

Can I include a trade-in?

Confirmed trade-in equity may contribute toward a purchase. This calculator does not appraise your car or account for an outstanding loan.

Is the rate shown available to me?

The APR is an example that you can change. A lender would determine actual eligibility, rate and terms.
